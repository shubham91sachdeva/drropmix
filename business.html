FAQ

Good questions.

What exactly is a Drrop Mix bomb?

A solid, effervescent drink bomb that dissolves in water or soda into a flavoured, fizzy drink. It comes in an individual pouch, three to a box.

Do I need to refrigerate it?

No. Drrop Mix does not need refrigeration. Store it in a cool, dry place. It has a 12-month shelf life.

How much liquid per bomb?

One bomb makes one drink in about 180 ml of chilled still or sparkling water. Venues can add their own spirit, mixer or garnish.

Is it alcohol-free?

Yes. The bomb itself is completely alcohol-free. Any spirit is an optional extra that a venue or adult guest adds, subject to your licence and local laws.

What are the claims on the label?

Drrop Mix carries "No artificial flavours or preservatives", "Crafted by mixologists" and "<5 g sugar" on its pack. The full ingredients and nutrition information are printed on every box.

What is the minimum order and price?

Trade pricing depends on quantity and delivery location. Tell us what you need in the form above and we'll come back with a quote.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Tick "sample kit" in the form or ask us on WhatsApp.

Quick check before we pour

Our recipes include optional spirits. Drrop Mix itself is completely alcohol-free. Are you of legal drinking age in your state?
