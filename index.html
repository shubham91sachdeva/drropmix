How it works

Drop. Fizz. Sip.

Scroll to watch it happen. Pour 180 ml of chilled water or soda, and you're done.

01

Drop

Tear open a pouch and drop one bomb into 180 ml of chilled still or sparkling water. Add 45 ml of your spirit if you like.

02

Fizz

Watch it sizzle. The bomb dissolves into flavour, bubbles and colour in seconds. Give it a quick stir.

03

Sip

Garnish, snap a photo, and sip. Repeat with the next flavour. Drrop. Mix. Impress.
